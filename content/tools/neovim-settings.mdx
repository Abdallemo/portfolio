My highly customized Neovim setup, built with Lua for maximum performance and extensibility.

### Key Focus Areas
- **Go Support**: Deep integration with `gopls` and `golangci-lint` for a seamless Go development experience.
- **TypeScript/Next.js**: Optimized for modern frontend development with `tsserver` and `eslint`.
- **Developer Experience**: Custom keybindings and status lines that match my "no-fluff" philosophy.

This configuration is the core of my daily coding workflow, ensuring that the editor stays out of the way while providing powerful IDE-like features.
