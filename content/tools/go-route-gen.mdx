I built **go-route-gen** to solve a specific pain point: the manual syncing of Go backend routes with TypeScript frontends. It's a Go AST scraper that extracts your standard `net/http` route definitions and generates a strictly-typed Axios client.

### Features
- **Strict Route Matching**: TypeScript won't let you request a route that doesn't exist in your backend.
- **Path Parameter Validation**: Automatically detects `{id}` segments in your Go routes.
- **Global Lifecycle Hooks**: Centralized `onError` and `onUnauthorized` handling.

This tool gives you tRPC-style type safety across the network boundary without having to switch to a heavy backend framework.
