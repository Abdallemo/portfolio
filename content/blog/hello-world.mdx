I've always spent a lot of time messing with computers and trying out new tools. I wanted to build a place where I could keep track of everything I'm working on, from my Linux setup to the random CLI tools I build during my internship.

### What this is for
This website serves as a digital notebook. My intention is not to instruct anyone—I'm merely a learner who enjoys experimenting and creating things. I sought a method to display my projects that accurately reflects the way I typically code, which is often in a terminal or a specially tailored editor.

### What you'll find here
- **Tinkering Logs**: I'll be posting about things I learn while building projects, like how I optimized a database query or how I'm setting up a new tool.
- **Projects**: Detailed looks at the bigger things I've built, like SolveIt or my ROS2 tools.
- **The Workbench**: A collection of the scripts and configs I use every day to stay productive.

I'm happy you're here to see what I'm up to. It's all a work in progress.
