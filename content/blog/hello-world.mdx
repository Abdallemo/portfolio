Welcome to the new version of my personal site. This is no longer just a landing page; it's my **Engineering Home Base**.

### Why the change?
As a developer who spends most of my time in the terminal, I wanted a site that reflects my workflow. But I also realized that a purely terminal-based site is hard to navigate and doesn't provide the utility that a modern dashboard can.

### What to expect
- **Deep Technical Logs**: Technical dives into Go, Arch Linux, and full-stack development.
- **Project Detail Pages**: Rich READMEs and architecture breakdowns for my most important work.
- **The Workbench**: A high-density view of the tools and dotfiles I use every day.

Thanks for stopping by.
