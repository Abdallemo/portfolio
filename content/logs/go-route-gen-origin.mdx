I got tired of manually syncing my Go standard library routes with my TypeScript frontend. The constant back-and-forth and the risk of typos in endpoint strings were frustrating.

I built **go-route-gen** to bridge this gap. It's a Go AST scraper that extracts `net/http` route patterns and generates a strictly-typed Axios client.

### Why not tRPC?
tRPC is great, but it requires switching to a specific ecosystem. I wanted something that works with standard `net/http` handlers and standard Axios.

### The result
- 100% type-safe endpoint strings.
- Path parameter validation at compile time.
- Automatic request/response typing.

It started as a small script to scratch my own itch, and now it's on NPM and getting organic downloads.
